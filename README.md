# Indian Insurance Equity Research & Valuation Platform

## Overview

An independent equity research project analyzing four listed Indian insurance companies using financial statement analysis, insurance-sector KPIs, forecasting, valuation techniques, and data analytics.

The objective is to evaluate how growth, profitability, business quality, and risk influence the valuation of insurance companies.

## Research Question

Which Indian insurance companies offer the most attractive valuation relative to their growth, profitability, and risk?

## Companies Covered

| Company | Segment | Key Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| HDFC Life Insurance | Life | APE, VNB, VNB Margin, Embedded Value |
| SBI Life Insurance | Life | APE, VNB, VNB Margin, Embedded Value |
| ICICI Lombard | General Insurance | GWP, Combined Ratio, Underwriting Profit |
| Star Health | Health Insurance | Premium Growth, Claims Ratio, Underwriting Performance |

## Planned Methodology

1. Collect historical financial statements, investor presentations, and market data.
2. Build a Python data-cleaning and validation pipeline.
3. Store standardized datasets in a SQL database.
4. Develop financial forecasts and company-specific valuation models in Excel.
5. Implement scenario analysis and selected VBA automation.
6. Create interactive dashboards to compare company performance and valuation.
7. Prepare a research report documenting assumptions, findings, and risks.

## Technology Stack

- Python: pandas, NumPy
- SQL: SQLite
- Microsoft Excel and VBA
- Tableau Public
- Git and GitHub

## Valuation Framework

**Life insurers:** Price-to-Embedded Value (P/EV), Price-to-Value of New Business (P/VNB), and relevant growth and profitability indicators.

**General and health insurers:** Price-to-Book (P/B), Price-to-Earnings (P/E), Return on Equity (RoE), combined ratio, and underwriting performance.

Valuation methods will be selected according to each company's business model and the availability and comparability of data.
